# NovaCart Customer Experience UI v9

This build upgrades the complete customer journey from sign-in/register through the storefront, product discovery, and full product detail view.

Highlights:
- Premium customer auth experience with a stronger visual hierarchy and benefits strip.
- Successful customer sign-in/register returns to the storefront homepage.
- Refined sticky header, search, navigation, hero, deal cards, category pills, and product cards.
- Product cards reveal a quick-view affordance and retain full-page product navigation.
- Full product page receives stronger premium spacing, trust blocks, purchase panel, reviews, and responsive behavior.
- Existing backend/auth/cart/checkout behavior is preserved.

Run the same way as the existing production project after extracting the ZIP and installing dependencies.
