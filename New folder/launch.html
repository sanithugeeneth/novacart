BEFORE YOU OPEN

Verify the customer journey.

  1. Confirm the public domain uses HTTPS, and save a database backup.
  2. Register, receive and open the verification email, then reset a password.
  3. Complete a Stripe test payment, check the order becomes paid and confirm its refund.
  4. Test each enabled sign-in provider and a real image search.
  5. Verify supplier access, the exact SKU and the accepted fulfillment reference. Confirm supplier payment and cancellation separately.
