Shipping & Returns

Delivery, cancellations, returns and refund guidance.

Shipping

Available shipping methods, costs and applicable free-shipping thresholds are calculated from the store configuration and shown at checkout.

Returns

The configured return window is shown by the store. Eligibility can depend on item condition, delivery status and mandatory consumer rights.

Refunds

Approved card refunds are returned through the payment provider. Cash-on-delivery refunds require the merchant’s configured refund process.

Order problems

For damaged, incorrect or missing items, contact support with the order number and relevant evidence as soon as possible.
